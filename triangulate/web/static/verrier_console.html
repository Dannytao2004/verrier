B-01 · ORIGIN1846
01 · The idea01 · 灵感

Found with a pen.用一支笔找到的行星。

In 1846 nobody had seen Neptune — and Urbain Le Verrier didn't need to. Uranus was drifting off course, and the drift could mean only one thing: something unseen was pulling on it. He worked out where it had to be and mailed the coordinates to Berlin.1846 年，没人见过海王星，勒威耶也不需要见到它。天王星总在偏离轨道，而这种偏离只说明一件事：有一个看不见的东西在拉它。他算出了它该在哪儿，把坐标寄往柏林。

Johann Galle pointed a telescope there the night the letter arrived, and found a new planet within one degree.伽勒在收到信的当晚把望远镜对准那里——在不到 1° 的地方，发现了一颗新行星。
