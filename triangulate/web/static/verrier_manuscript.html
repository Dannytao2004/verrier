Historia

Caput I · The idea第一章 · 灵感

Found with a pen.用一支笔
找到的行星。

1846 — Le Verrier pinpointed Neptune from the wobble of Uranus, before anyone had seen it.1846 年，勒威耶仅凭天王星的“摆动”，在任何人看到它之前就锁定了海王星。

IIn 1846 nobody had seen Neptune — and Urbain Le Verrier didn’t need to. Uranus was drifting off course, and the drift could mean only one thing: something unseen was pulling on it. He worked out where it had to be and mailed the coordinates to Berlin.一一八四六年，没人见过海王星，勒威耶也不需要见到它。天王星总在偏离轨道，而这种偏离只说明一件事：有一个看不见的东西在拉它。他算出了它该在哪儿，把坐标寄往柏林。

Johann Galle pointed a telescope there the night the letter arrived, and found a new planet within one degree.伽勒在收到信的当晚把望远镜对准那里——在不到 1° 的地方，发现了一颗新行星。

the drift that betrayed it就是这一点偏差，出卖了它

Verrier brings the same idea to data: the numbers that matter leave traces in the numbers that are public.Verrier 把同样的思路带进数据世界：重要的数字，总会在公开的数字里留下痕迹。

Methodus

Caput II · From question to answer第二章 · 从问题到答案

Ask. Approve. Get the evidence.提问，确认，拿到证据。

One question in. One defensible number out.输入一个问题，输出一个经得起追问的数字。

  1. Prop. I.

    Ask提问

    Name the number you need — next month’s births, this quarter’s deliveries, a figure no one reports.说出你要的数字：下个月的出生数、本季度的交付量、没人统计的数据。

  2. Prop. II.

    Approve确认

    Verrier drafts the estimation routes and sources. You sign off before a single search runs.Verrier 先拿出推算路线和数据来源，你批准之后才开始搜索。7

  3. Prop. III.

    Gather搜集

    Parallel agents read the public record and bring back numbers with verbatim quotes.多个 agent 并行阅读公开记录，带回附原文引用的数字。

  4. Prop. IV.

    Compute计算

    Code — not the model — runs the math: formulas, Monte Carlo, weights earned in backtests.计算交给代码而不是模型：公式、Monte Carlo、按回测表现加权。

  5. Prop. V.

    Deliver交付

    An estimate with a range, an evidence ledger and a dependency graph you can audit.带区间的估计，加上可审计的证据账本和依赖图。

Closer than asking the model.比直接问模型更准。
